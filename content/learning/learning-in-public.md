## Why this matters

Learning becomes more durable when it leaves traces. A short note, a diagram, a tiny summary, or even a list of questions can make an idea easier to revisit later.

## Current rule

- Capture the idea while it is fresh.
- Rewrite it once in my own words.
- Attach it to a project, decision, or question.

> The goal is not to look finished. The goal is to make progress visible.

## Next experiment

I want each study session to end with one reusable artifact: a paragraph, snippet, checklist, or question. Small enough to keep doing, clear enough to build on.
