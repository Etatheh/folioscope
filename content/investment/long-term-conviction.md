## The useful kind of conviction

Conviction is not stubbornness. It is the result of doing enough work that short-term noise becomes easier to interpret.

## Questions before acting

- What would make this thesis wrong?
- How much downside can I emotionally and financially tolerate?
- Is this decision based on process or mood?

## Reminder

A good investment journal should record both the thesis and the emotional weather around the decision.
