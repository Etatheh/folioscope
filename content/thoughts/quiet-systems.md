## Systems should lower friction

The best personal systems do not create another identity to perform. They simply make the next good action easier to take.

## What I want

- A place for ideas before they are polished.
- A structure that supports review without becoming heavy.
- A rhythm that survives busy weeks.

Maybe the right system is not the one that captures everything. Maybe it is the one I keep returning to.
